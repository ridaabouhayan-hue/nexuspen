NexusPen 1.3.8 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.8**
- **Timer:** typing your own time now works. Click the digits, type for example `5`, `5:30` or `90s`, then press Enter.
- **Screenshot:** if a folder is locked (for example by Windows security or OneDrive), NexusPen saves in Downloads > NexusPen instead. You always get a message, also when it fails.
- **Esc:** the first Esc gives you a normal pointer. Pressing Esc again hides nothing.
- **Hiding:** the button that hid your drawings is gone. Only collapsing the toolbar hides everything, and only the bin clears everything.
- **Settings** opens on top of the board, and the board stays open.
