NexusPen 1.3.17 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.17**
- **Calm timer sounds:** five new sounds of about 4.5 seconds that calmly tell everyone the time is up: singing bowl, three tones, piano, harp and bells. Choose one in Settings > Timer or with a right-click on the timer button. You hear it right away as an example.
- **How often:** once, twice, three times, or until you click the timer (30 seconds at most).
- **Sharper text** in the right-click menu.
