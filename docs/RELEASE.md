NexusPen 1.3.15 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.15**
- **Projector or second screen:**
  - When you extend your screen and something is drawn, NexusPen offers to move your drawing to the other screen. You can also do it from the logo menu or Settings > Screen sharing.
  - Settings > Screen sharing > "Drawing and board on": choose one screen. The other screens stay free, so you keep working in your own programs there, even with the board open.
- **Timer:** choose a sound in Settings > Timer (small bell, alarm clock, notification or gong). Turn the sound off with the speaker button in the timer itself.
- **Right-click a button** in the toolbar for its options: chalk and thickness for the pen, laser mode, font and size for text, eraser size, bright or soft colours, which stamp, board and background, timer sound, and the screenshot folder.
- **Settings:** no more floating tips where the explanation is already shown.
