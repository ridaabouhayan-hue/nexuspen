NexusPen 1.3.10 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.10**
- **Board:** when you open the toolbar again, the board and its text come back at the same moment.
- **Windows of the toolbar** (colours, shapes, stamps, board): click the same button again to close it. The window stays open while you work in the toolbar, and closes by itself as soon as you click somewhere else, so it never covers your work.
