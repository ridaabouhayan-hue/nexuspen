NexusPen 1.3.6 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.6**
- **English is now the default language.** Existing users keep their language.
- **Five new languages:** Portuguese, Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Chinese.
- **What's new** is always in English.
- **Clear confirmation** with a green check after Save and Screenshot.
- **Fading time** only shows when fading is on.
