NexusPen 1.3.12 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.12**
- **Three more fonts** in Settings > Text: Arial, Calibri and Comic Sans. They come with Windows.
- **Delete** at the end of the text you are typing removes the whole text box. Undo brings it back.
- **The blinking cursor** is black again, like in Windows. On a dark board it is white.
