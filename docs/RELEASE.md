NexusPen 1.3.19 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.19**
- **Better undo while typing:** Ctrl+Z goes back word by word. When there is nothing left to undo in the text, Ctrl+Z goes further back, like in Word. Clicked somewhere else with Text and pressed Ctrl+Z? Then the text you just wrote comes back off, instead of nothing happening.
- **Board only on the other screen:** with a projector, the board window has a button **Only on other screen**. The board and drawing then show only on the projector, and you keep working on your own screen. Also in the right-click menu of the board button and in Settings > Board.
