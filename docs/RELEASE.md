NexusPen 1.3.13 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.13**
- **Outline around lines and shapes:** pen, chalk, line, arrow, rectangle and circle. Turn it on in Settings > Text, with the same thickness and colour as the text outline. Select one line or shape to turn its outline on or off.
- **No extra click:** when you go back to the normal mouse, the program you were working in is active again right away.
- **Usage (only on this PC):** per stroke a few numbers: tool, colour number, thickness, duration, length, screen or board. Never where, what shape, or what you write. Settings > Usage > Export for Claude puts it in one file on your desktop.
