NexusPen 1.3.9 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.9**
- **Screenshots work again**, with your drawing and the board on them. They are saved in Pictures > NexusPen and copied to the clipboard, and you see a green check.

**Also new since 1.3.7**
- **Timer:** typing your own time works. Click the digits, type for example `5`, `5:30` or `90s`, then press Enter.
- **Esc** gives a normal pointer. Pressing it again hides nothing.
- **Hiding:** only collapsing the toolbar hides everything, and only the bin clears everything.
- **Settings** opens on top of the board.
