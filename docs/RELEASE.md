NexusPen 1.3.14 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.14**
- **Duplicate or extend your screen** (for example with a projector): NexusPen now covers every screen completely again. It checks a few times while Windows switches, and keeps checking in the background.
- **The board on more than one screen:** each screen gets its own lines, margins and zoom, in the scale of that screen.
