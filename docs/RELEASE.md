NexusPen 1.3.18 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.18**
- **Toolbar any size you like:** right-click the logo and choose **Change size** (or Settings > Appearance). The toolbar gets a coloured border: drag it or turn the mouse wheel. Click **Done**, or click somewhere else, to keep the size. Esc goes back to how it was. The windows that open from the toolbar grow and shrink along.
- The size only changes when you turn this on yourself, so it never changes by accident during a lesson.
