NexusPen 1.3.11 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.11**
- **Send to back:** next to Bring to front, so you can reach something small that lies under something big.
- **Right-click in text** while typing: Cut, Copy, Paste and Select all, with their shortcuts.
- **Typing looks normal:** no special round handle any more. Move the text by its frame, make it bigger or smaller with the corner. The blinking cursor has the colour of your text.
- **One kind of frame** for selecting and for typing, in the accent colour of your theme.
