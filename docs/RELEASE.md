NexusPen 1.3.16 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.16**
- **Board button:** click it to close the board and go back to your screen. Click again and the board of last time opens. Esc closes the board too.
- **Settings > Board:** choose what the board button opens, and whether what is on the board is kept or cleared when you close it.
- **Delete a page** of the board with the bin in the board window.
- **Timer:** make it any size with the mouse wheel or by dragging the corner, and a real full screen button (Esc = back).
- **Fonts** in the right-click menu of Text are shown in their own letters. Verdana replaces Comic Sans.
- **Magnifier:** turn the mouse pointer off (right-click the magnifier button, or Settings > Pen and colours).
