NexusPen 1.3.20 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.20**
- **Undo and redo are kept:** the screen and every page of the board each have their own Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y). Switching pages, or closing and opening the board, no longer throws away what you can redo.
