NexusPen 1.3.21 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.21**
- **Move text while typing:** press and hold the mouse button in the text for about half a second without moving. The pointer becomes the four arrows: now drag and the whole text box moves along. Let go and you keep typing.
- A normal click or drag still works as before: clicking places the cursor, dragging selects words, and selected words can be dragged. Grabbing the edge still moves the text right away.

**Also in 1.3.20**
- Undo and redo are kept for the screen and for every board page, also when you switch pages or close and open the board.
