NexusPen 1.3.23 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.23**
- **Typing line and selection in the right place:** the blinking line and the blue selection now sit exactly on the letters, also at the end of a long line.
- **Menus close a bit sooner** when your mouse leaves them.
- Moving text while typing: hold still for a little less long (0.4 seconds), and a small wobble of the mouse is fine.

**Also in 1.3.22**
- **Menus close by themselves:** move your mouse away from a menu (for example the right-click menu of a button) and it closes after a little over half a second. Not right away, so you can move calmly to an item or briefly pass the edge.

**Also in 1.3.21**
- Move text while typing: press and hold in the text for half a second, then drag.
