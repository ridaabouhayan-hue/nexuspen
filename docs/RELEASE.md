NexusPen 1.3.26 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.26**
- **Calligraphy pen:** thick and thin strokes, like writing with a slanted nib. Right-click the pen button and choose Calligraphy pen (or in Settings > Pen and colours).
- **Pressure sensitivity:** with a pen on a tablet or touchscreen, press lightly for a thin line and firmly for a thick one. With the mouse nothing changes. On by default.
- **Smooth line:** a shaky hand or mouse still gives a calm line. Turn it on in the pen's right-click menu.
- **Cleaner menus:** right-click menus now have a small heading, roomier rows and calmer shortcut hints.

**Also in 1.3.25**
- **Shortcuts that always work:** while you draw, every NexusPen shortcut works, whichever program is in front. If you want, Ctrl+Shift + letter (or number) also picks a tool or colour while you work in another program: turn on Settings > Shortcuts > Also in other programs. It is off by default, because those keys then no longer work in that program (for example Ctrl+Shift+M to mute in Teams). NexusPen only reacts to its own shortcuts and never keeps anything you type.
- **Colour picks your tool:** with the mouse pointer or the eraser, picking a colour switches back to the tool you used last.
- **Draw in the magnifier:** draw inside a pinned magnifier and the ink lands on the spot it magnifies (right-click the magnifier button to turn it off).
- **Chalk feels like chalk:** a rougher grain with small gaps and a soft dusty edge.
- **Square spotlight**, next to round and the reading strip.
- **The collapsed logo keeps its size** when you switch between sharing, duplicating or extending your screen.

**Earlier: 1.3.24**
- **Timer on the whole screen:** the full screen button now fills the entire screen. Choose a circle that runs down, digits only, or digits with a bar (the button next to reset).
- **Timer to the other screen:** drag it there, or use the screen button on the full screen timer. It keeps its size, also on a screen with another scale.
- **Changing screens works properly:** extend, duplicate or unplug a beamer and the toolbar keeps your own size (it only gets smaller for a while when a screen is too small). Your drawing stays on the same screen and scales along; when a screen disappears it comes to your main screen.
- **Magnifier up to 10 times.**

**Earlier: 1.3.23**
- **Typing line and selection in the right place:** the blinking line and the blue selection now sit exactly on the letters, also at the end of a long line.
- **Menus close a bit sooner** when your mouse leaves them.
- Moving text while typing: hold still for a little less long (0.4 seconds), and a small wobble of the mouse is fine.

**Earlier: 1.3.22**
- **Menus close by themselves:** move your mouse away from a menu (for example the right-click menu of a button) and it closes after a little over half a second. Not right away, so you can move calmly to an item or briefly pass the edge.

**Also in 1.3.21**
- Move text while typing: press and hold in the text for half a second, then drag.
