NexusPen 1.3.7 for Windows 10 and 11.

**Install:** update with one click (Settings > Updates). Or download `NexusPen-Setup.exe` and open it. Your drawings and settings stay.

**New in 1.3.7**
- **Pointer on a board:** clicking the pointer only gives you a normal mouse pointer. The board and your drawing stay. Only the logo (or Esc twice) hides everything.
- **Timer:** click the digits and type your own time, for example `5`, `5:30` or `90s`, then press Enter.
